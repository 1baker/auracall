## Planning Authority Contract

| Concern | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Priority, lane selection, and sequencing | `ROADMAP.md` |

active plan, but every plan it lists must be active. Roadmap `Active` links must
also resolve to active canonical plans.

## Historical Decisions Retained

- Planning migration was structural first, then semantic. Promoting a file into
  `docs/dev/plans/` did not prove that its content still matched shipped state.
- Completed and superseded plans remain durable history rather than being
  deleted.
- The plan index remains curated; completeness is not silently inferred from
  its name.
- Product behavior, provider work, broad legacy normalization, and roadmap
  reprioritization were outside the migration slice.
- The long historical execution ledger that accumulated below the original
  plan is preserved in `RUNBOOK.md`, successor plans, and git history rather
  than duplicated in this terminal authority.
